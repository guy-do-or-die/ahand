# ADR-0015: Core v2 — any ERC-20 per Hand, optional donation, unbounded routes, generic settlement facts, rollback push frame

Status: proposed (awaiting the owner's yes at freeze)

Numbering: 0014 is intentionally unassigned (0009 never existed); the gap is recorded in [README.md](README.md). This ADR is the one supersession record for the Core v2 change (H30): nothing in it is a silent exception, and every affected document under `docs/` is updated in the same change.

## Context

The accepted ADRs describe the hackathon Core (branch baseline d6bb8c9): one immutable stablecoin per deployment valued at $1, a mandatory 1–30 % donation, `MAX_SHAKES = 6`, one SHAKEN receipt per distinct account, `uint96` amounts and `uint8` positions, a boolean push whose success is the token's return value, a `ThankPermit` typehash never implemented, and product calendar bounds in Core. Between 2026-09 and 2026-11 the owner decided otherwise, item by item (the D-numbered decisions in [core-v2-requirements.md](../20-protocol/core-v2-requirements.md) §2): any ERC-20 per Hand with `uint256` pools (D7, D1), no hop constant with one atomic Thank and the chain's gas as the only bound (D3, H7), donation optional including 0 % with the 30 % ceiling kept (D4, D34), zero-reward Hands (D8), one receipt per occurrence (D13), native Down without a fixed cost (D14), receipts outside Thank (D16), no sponsor path (D17), the owner's single key as policyAdmin (D18), the controller-as-Raiser seam (D23), Core as a recorder of facts (D24), and "seamless extension by any scenario over a frozen Core" (D21). The research rounds added findings the old code does not meet: a transfer-then-false token double-counts (S1), a surplus can mask a double payment (S2), a sender-fee token enters and then fails every exit (S3), a Raiser can under-fund gas to park every payout as a claim, and a Signals-shaped commitment lets no second contract verify a settlement (S15, S16, S22).

On 2026-11-01 the owner wrote the goal statement for this step («привести контракты к виду, который мы в итоге задумали … расширяемость, модульность, универсальность … с неизменяемым, непробиваемым ядром … каждая строчка на своём месте, никакого слопа, узких мест и уязвимостей … решай сам, как будет лучше») and delegated the choices the design had left open. The design that answers all of this is [core-v2-design.md](../20-protocol/core-v2-design.md); it supersedes clauses of seven accepted ADRs and restates two hard invariants, which is what forces this ADR.

## Decision

Deploy Core v2 as specified in [core-v2-design.md](../20-protocol/core-v2-design.md) and built on branch `core-v2`: one immutable `AHandCore` with exactly four externally reachable mutating entry points (`raise`, `thank`, `reclaim`, `withdraw`) plus one self-only rollback frame (`pushExact`), a frozen `AHandTypes`/`AHandSig`/`AHandFacts` surface, the EIP-712 domain `("aHand", "3")`, the versioned generic settlement commitment `aHand.settlement.v2` with per-hop allocations and the terminal time, the narrow `IAHandFacts` read interface, exact-delta custody on both sides of every deposit and inside a reverting frame for every push, the `InsufficientGas` starvation guard, hop-indexed errors over a `Hop[]`/`Terminal` ABI, and the bounded prospective policyAdmin unchanged. `AHandSignalsV2` ships beside it as the reference reader of the facts seam with a constructor-fixed USDC-only qualification profile. Extension is by composition at three seams (participation, opaque typed data, authenticated facts) and never by a call Core makes.

### Why the frame, and why H8/H2 are restated rather than widened

The alternative to `pushExact` is an inline delta-trusting push: a gas-capped `call` to `token.transfer` with `out = 0`, the return word ignored, and the custody delta alone deciding (`Δ == amount` pushed, `Δ == 0` deferred, anything else reverts the whole thank). It needs no fifth selector and no self-call, and for a **non-conforming** token the two are equivalent (a token whose exits are inexact is a total stop under either). For a **conforming** token with recipient hooks (ERC-777-style `tokensReceived`, or any token that calls the recipient on transfer) a hostile recipient can send part of the amount back to Core during the push, and for a token with a selective fee one listed recipient yields a partial custody delta while the token stays exact for everyone else. Under the inline push that partial delta must revert the whole thank (nothing undoes a partial move without a frame), so one consented hostile hop kills the Hand's settlement for everyone — the giver loses and only reclaim after expiry remains: the bottleneck the owner forbids. Under the frame the anomaly rolls back inside `pushExact` and only that recipient's share parks as a claim, so "a failing push parks exactly its own share" (H10) holds for every conforming token. The frame is therefore kept, and because H8 and H2 are "never violated" invariants and not ADR clauses, they are restated as H8'/H2' in the open rather than widened by an exception. Reversing before freeze costs ≈ 10 lines (drop `pushExact`, `_pushFrame`, `OnlySelf`; switch `_pay` to the inline push; re-cut the selector-inventory and I-18 tests) at the price above.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Every owner decision since the hackathon is in code once, traceable by id to the ledger, and nothing was ported from v0 without re-argument (H29).
- A second contract can verify a settlement without an indexer: `facts()` plus the `aHand.settlement.v2` commitment with per-hop allocations and the terminal time; Signals v2 does exactly this and Core never learns it exists.
- Exact deltas on both sides of a deposit reject sender-fee and burn tokens at the door; every push is exact or nothing, parks exactly its own share, and cannot be starved by the caller's gas choice, so `eth_estimateGas` is sound.
- Extension needs no hook, registry or admin: controllers, pools, agents, policy capabilities, Safes, splitters, tip distributors, voting governors and valuation readers attach at the three seams, each proved on the unchanged candidate by a probe in the extensions suite.
- The frozen surface is small and audited: OZ `EIP712`, `ReentrancyGuardTransient`, `ECDSA.tryRecoverCalldata`, `SafeERC20`, `Math.mulDiv`, `SafeCast` instead of hand-written Yul; `AHandCore` is 13 176 bytes of runtime.

### Negative

- A fifth selector exists (`pushExact`), reachable only by Core itself; H8 and H2 read as H8'/H2' from now on.
- Shared custody per token means a token that turns hostile after entry (late sender fee, Core blacklisted or paused) stops every exit of that token, the reclaim refund included; nothing in Core can repair it.
- Thank calldata, not gas, bounds honest EOA routes at 226 explicit hops through a public mempool; wallet-signed paid routes are bounded by gas at ≈ 206–209 hops; the UI must preflight both.
- Every v1 signature, vector and SDK envelope dies with the domain version; the subgraph must index the widened payout events and the policy events.
- The Up gate, the valuation profile and the Down eligibility are Signals deployment facts, not Core facts: changing any of them is a new Signals.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Inline delta-trusting push (no frame):** one consented hostile hop under a conforming hook or selective-fee token reverts the whole thank; see above.
- **Per-Hand vault (Q2 option B):** a CREATE2 clone per raise and a hop through it on every push, a permanent tax for isolation the entry rule already provides; cannot help against a dishonest `balanceOf`, a late fee or a blacklist; a second contract in the money path.
- **Pure pull settlement, staged pre-verification, a hop cap:** rejected by D3/N5; the practical limit is measured instead.
- **Ownerless or frozen-allowlist Core:** ADR-0012:55 rejected it; no owner decision asks for it.
- **Mutable token-qualification curator in Signals:** a hidden valuation registry (D26, N9, H15).
- **A `raiseOpen` admission switch:** a key that can halt every new Raise and cannot stop 0 %-donation Raises anyway.
- **Merkle settlement commitment, stored giver and per-hop allocations, a global solvency gate:** cost without a consumer; the flat preimage and the frame cover them.
- **Hooks, callbacks, a plugin registry, delegatecall, pause, rescue, redirect:** each would let periphery block or redirect money (H1, H2, N4, N10).

## Revisit when

The owner says yes (status becomes accepted and the three gas constants, the domain version and the `aHand.settlement.v2` tag freeze), or reverses a §18 row (the row names its cost); a conforming token class appears for which the frame's exact-or-nothing rule is the wrong promise; Base changes its per-transaction gas cap or txpool `txMaxSize` (the route limits are re-measured); a second Core deployment is wanted on another chain (row 3 is per deployment); or a Seam-3 reader needs a fact the commitment does not carry (a new `SETTLEMENT_TAG` and a new Core, never a silent widening).
